I didn’t climb a career ladder. I built one. Across 9.5 years at the largest auto transport broker in the U.S., every flagship win started the same way: no playbook, no precedent, no dedicated owner. A feedback infrastructure, a voice-of-the-customer program, a fraud detection workflow, a UX function, and a first-of-its-kind guaranteed service all went from zero to operational because I made them exist.

The proof is in the systems, not the slides: a guaranteed service that runs roughly 10% of annual revenue at 35% fewer inbound contacts, a public rating rebuilt from 2.5 stars that was cited in the 2020 acquisition and later sustained at 4.7, a fraud framework that mitigated $250k+ in liability and became its own department, and an experimentation practice good for an 18% conversion lift across ~30 tests.

And for my last two years, I made AI a daily operating layer. I called Google’s AI Overviews shift early and built the synthesis and scoring systems that turned raw signal into decisions.

These case studies include what worked, the real numbers, and honest retrospectives on what I’d do differently. If a case study reads like a highlight reel, it isn’t telling you the truth.
